SmartPack - SmartPack was a project me and a team of 3 others built during our time at the UH HFB Hackathon.
SmartPack is a passive computer-vision inspector for food box packing stations. It first learns an empty-table reference,
waits for a box to enter and settle, stabilizes the visible item count, then records that count when the box is removed. It
loads the next station model and repeats the process
